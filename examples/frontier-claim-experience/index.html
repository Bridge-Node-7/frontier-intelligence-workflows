04 · Challenge

What does the evidence support—and what does it still leave open?

What Supports This?

What Challenges This?

No independent lot-level qualification record is present in the synthetic evidence set.

Critical Unknown

Whether independent production qualification under intended-use conditions has been completed.

Most Decision-Relevant Missing Evidence

Independent lot-level qualification evidence under intended-use conditions.

  • Decision relevance: directly addresses production dependency.
  • Discrimination: distinguishes repeated supplier-origin reporting from independently observed readiness.
  • Reversibility: requesting evidence preserves optionality before deeper commitment.
