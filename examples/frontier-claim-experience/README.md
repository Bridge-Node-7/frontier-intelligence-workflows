# Frontier Claim Experience — Synthetic Local-First Experience

This public-safe synthetic experience demonstrates one bounded FIW journey:

**Decision → Claim → Evidence → Source Lineage → Critical Unknown → Decision-Relevant Missing Evidence → Human Decision → Exact Artifact**

Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser. No server, package install, account, network service, external font, analytics endpoint, or backend is required.

## What the case demonstrates

Three cited reports appear to support a fictional production-readiness claim. Tracing provenance shows that all three reports resolve to one supplier-origin root, so independent corroboration is not established. The case then exposes the critical missing evidence and preserves the final decision as a human-owned, scoped judgment.

## Boundaries

- The case is entirely fictional and public-safe.
- The interface does not determine truth, qualify a supplier, authorize deployment, or provide investment advice.
- The lineage relationships are explicit case data; the browser does not invent them.
- Session decisions exist in memory only and are not transmitted or persisted.
- `HOLD` is a legitimate human disposition, not a failure state.
- The canonical synthetic artifact is `../../data/synthetic/frontier-claim-experience.json`; the HTML embeds a regression-tested identical copy for direct-file offline use.
- The exact synthetic artifact remains inspectable inside the experience.

This experience is intentionally narrow. It is not a general FIW platform, collaboration product, AI agent, or production deployment.
