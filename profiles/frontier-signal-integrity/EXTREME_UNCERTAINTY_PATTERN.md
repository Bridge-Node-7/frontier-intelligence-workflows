# Extreme-Uncertainty Investigation Pattern

Frontier Signal Integrity may be used when a consequential claim combines high novelty, incomplete access, uncertain provenance, strategic surprise, or strong narrative pressure.

The pattern does not determine whether a claim is true or false. It protects the boundary between possibility and knowledge.

## Entry conditions

Use the pattern when material questions involve:

- uncertain source independence;
- mixed testimony and physical evidence;
- a supported fact extended into a stronger conclusion;
- moving predictions;
- underdefined failure conditions;
- symbolic interpretation treated as physical evidence;
- restricted evidence treated as proof of its contents;
- repeated reporting treated as independent corroboration.

## Preserve the signal

Record what was actually observed, measured, or reported before interpreting it.

Keep source identity, time, mode, attribution, uncertainty, and limitations visible.

## Separate evidence layers

Keep direct observation, measurement, testimony, calculation, inference, interpretation, model output, hypothesis, and symbolic meaning distinct.

A credible source may still support only a narrow proposition.

## Competing hypotheses

Novel explanations are allowed.

Conventional and null alternatives remain required when plausible.

A useful hypothesis set allows evidence to distinguish serious alternatives.

## Unsupported extension

A supported anchor does not automatically establish a stronger adjacent claim.

Identify the missing bridge evidence and the smallest test that might provide it.

## Source dependence

Publication count is not source independence.

Use Source Genealogy before treating repeated reports as corroboration.

Unknown genealogy remains unknown.

## Prediction integrity

Freeze exact predictions, observation windows, success criteria, failure criteria, ambiguity criteria, and unresolved criteria before the resolution window.

Later changes require an update receipt. Preserve the original.

## Discriminating tests

Prefer evidence that separates competing hypotheses over evidence merely compatible with the preferred explanation.

More data is not automatically more useful evidence.

## Symbolic boundary

Cultural, spiritual, historical, or symbolic interpretation may remain part of the case as interpretation.

It does not silently become physical measurement or causal evidence.

## Restricted-evidence boundary

A real access restriction establishes that access is limited.

It does not by itself establish the contents of what cannot be inspected.

Preserve the access fact separately from any content hypothesis.

## AI boundary

AI may generate hypotheses, alternatives, missing-evidence questions, and candidate tests.

Generated novelty receives no evidentiary weight merely because a model proposed it.

Human review remains required.

## Decision boundary

A case does not need universal resolution before supporting a bounded decision.

The evidence may be sufficient to decide whether to research, test, publish, approach a partner, or reopen an earlier assessment.

Decision sufficiency is not universal truth.

## Public example boundary

Public examples should remain fictional, synthetic, or explicitly public-safe.

The reusable method should survive when the case name disappears.

## Success criterion

The pattern succeeds when a difficult claim remains explorable without allowing repetition, testimony, restricted access, symbolism, or generated hypotheses to become stronger evidence than they actually are.
