# Radiant Guardian Method v0.1

## 1. Preserve the signal

Record what was actually observed, measured, or reported. Do not begin by rewriting it into the preferred explanation.

## 2. Establish genealogy

Identify roots, descendants, republications, commentary, summaries, AI derivations, and unknown lineage. Repetition does not create independence.

## 3. Expand competing hypotheses

Require a serious alternative set. Novel hypotheses are allowed; null and conventional explanations are not optional merely because they are less exciting.

## 4. Identify reasoning hazards

Flag, at minimum:

- `REAL_ANCHOR_EXTRAORDINARY_EXTENSION`
- `SELF_SEALING_RISK`
- `ARGUMENT_FROM_SECRECY`
- `SOURCE_DEPENDENCY`
- `PREDICTION_DRIFT_RISK`
- `UNFALSIFIABLE_OR_UNDERDEFINED`

A reasoning-pattern flag is not proof that a claim is false. It identifies a condition requiring stronger evidence design.

## 5. Freeze predictions

Before the resolution window, record the exact statement, window, success criteria, failure criteria, ambiguity criteria, and unresolved criteria. Later correction requires a receipt; the original remains historical.

## 6. Design discriminating tests

Do not merely ask whether evidence is compatible with one hypothesis. Ask what observable result would distinguish the serious alternatives.

## 7. Protect hypothesis/evidence separation

Human, AI-assisted, Creative-Psionics, or source-derived hypotheses remain hypotheses. Generated novelty is not evidence.

## 8. Update with receipts

Material belief changes preserve the previous state, new state, evidence delta, rationale, reviewer, timestamp, and reversal trigger.

## 9. Track strategic surprise

For consequential cases, state what is expected, what would disconfirm the model, what would surprise the team, and when the case must be reviewed again.

## 10. Close the Proof Loop

After decisions/actions, record outcomes and method lessons. Do not rewrite the historical forecast to fit the result.
