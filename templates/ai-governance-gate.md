## Assistance used

## Source control

- Are sources preserved and identifiable?
- Are observed facts separated from generated inference?
- Were citations and calculations checked by a human?

## Public boundary

- Is the content synthetic, sanitized, or approved for public release?
- Does it exclude credentials, private data, proprietary methods, supplier intelligence, patent strategy, and controlled information?

## Claim control

- Are confidence and limitations visible?
- Does the artifact avoid unsupported validation, qualification, certification, or readiness claims?

## Human decision

Approve / Approve with limitations / Revise / Do not release.

## Rationale and conditions

Record the decision, required corrections, and accountable human reviewer.
