## Bottom line

State the current supportable disposition or next move first. If the evidence does not support commitment, state that constraint first.

## Why

Summarize the few decision-relevant facts that support the bottom line. Keep source identifiers available for inspection.

## Prepared next move

State the bounded action or proof step supported by the current evidence. This is advisory preparation and does not replace accountable human judgment.

## Strongest countercase

State the strongest material reason not to take the prepared move.

## Decision hinge

State the fact, assumption, threshold, or dependency most likely to change the current disposition.

## Decision criteria and tradeoffs

List the criteria that materially distinguish the options. Separate evidence-backed consequences from owner-set priorities, thresholds, lawful constraints, and value judgments. Preserve material dissent or affected-party perspectives when they change the decision basis. Do not manufacture weights or a single optimal choice when the evidence does not uniquely determine one.

## Minimum sufficient commitment

When a full commitment is not justified, state the smallest reversible action that can materially reduce decision uncertainty. Include time, capital, or coordination burden only when supported by evidence or explicit assumptions.

## Current evidence

Summarize the strongest supporting and contradictory evidence with source IDs.

## What is established

List only findings supported within the documented scope.

## What is not established

List unresolved qualification, certification, readiness, performance, safety, legal, commercial, or operational questions.

## Critical unknowns

List unresolved facts that could materially change the decision.

## Options

Describe realistic options, including a hold, no-action, or stop option when appropriate.

## Cost of action / cost of waiting

State material consequences only when supported by evidence or explicit assumptions. Otherwise mark them unknown.

## Risks and stop conditions

State the principal risks and the conditions that require pause, reversal, or escalation.

## Reconsider when

State the evidence, event, threshold, or changed dependency that should reopen the decision.

## Recommendation

State the smallest justified next action in ordinary language. Keep the recommendation distinct from the accountable human decision.
